# IEEE Internet of Things Journal submission sources checked

Checked: 2026-09-24

1. IEEE Internet of Things Journal - Guidelines for Authors
   https://ieee-iotj.org/guidelines-for-authors/
   - Original/substantial work; no concurrent review elsewhere
   - IEEE double-column journal format
   - Abstract: 150-250 words, one paragraph, no citations or displayed equations
   - ORCID required for all authors
   - Author-selected journal keywords/classification
   - Graphical abstract optional; if submitted, it is peer reviewed
   - $175 mandatory charge for each published page beyond the first eight
   - Hybrid journal: Traditional publication has no OA APC; OA option has an APC

2. IEEE Author Center - Submission and Peer Review Policies
   https://journals.ieeeauthorcenter.ieee.org/become-an-ieee-journal-author/publishing-ethics/guidelines-and-policies/submission-and-peer-review-policies/
   - Prior/similar publications and submissions must be disclosed and cited
   - Significant extension from prior publication must be clearly stated
   - Human-subject work requires IRB/ethics oversight statement or explanation, plus consent statement or explanation
   - AI-generated content must be disclosed in Acknowledgment, identifying the AI system, affected sections, and level of use

3. IEEE Author Center - The IEEE Article Submission Process
   https://journals.ieeeauthorcenter.ieee.org/submit-your-article-for-peer-review/the-ieee-article-submission-process/

4. IEEE Author Center - Tools for IEEE Authors
   https://journals.ieeeauthorcenter.ieee.org/create-your-ieee-journal-article/authoring-tools-and-templates/tools-for-ieee-authors/
   - LaTeX Analyzer
   - Reference Preparation Assistant
   - PDF Checker
