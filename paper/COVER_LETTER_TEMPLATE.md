# Cover letter template - IEEE Internet of Things Journal

Dear Editor-in-Chief,

Please consider our manuscript, **"UATimer: Lightweight Context-Aware Power Management for Heterogeneous IoT Systems,"** for publication in the IEEE Internet of Things Journal.

The manuscript presents a lightweight user-space power-management framework for heterogeneous IoT and mobile-edge systems. UATimer adapts idle thresholds from observed inter-event behavior while separating timeout smoothing from context-dependent energy-delay policy weights. The implementation requires fewer than 500 lines of code and less than 1 KB of policy state and does not require kernel modification. The evaluation reports energy, event latency, and AR/VR quality indicators across representative interactive and sensing workloads.

The manuscript fits the journal's scope in IoT enabling technologies, systematic integration, constrained devices, and IoT trials/experiments. The work emphasizes deployability on resource-constrained devices and the energy-responsiveness trade-off of runtime power management.
